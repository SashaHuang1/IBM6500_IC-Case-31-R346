1 Study overview

The EFCU questionnaire assesses members’ perceptions of employees, services, and operations. This report uses the 128 records in the supplied cleaned SPSS file. Descriptive results are presented first, followed by inferential analyses. Statistical significance is evaluated at alpha = .05. All analyses are exploratory and observational; differences do not establish causality.

Coding note. The SPSS file contains previously recoded Q9_R, Q10_R, Q13_R–Q26_R and Q37_R variables. Their existing numeric codes are used. The neutral midpoint of five-point agreement items is 3. For chi-square goodness-of-fit tests, the null is an equal distribution across the observed response categories; this is a descriptive benchmark, not a substantive population expectation. Q12 = 3 is the ‘No answer’ category, not a substantive ‘No’.

1.14 R4b — Awareness of services by residence

The table compares the percentage who were aware of and had used each service (response code 1). Each service also receives a separate Pearson chi-square test across all three awareness categories (used, aware/not used, unaware). At the unadjusted .05 level, no individual service meets the .05 threshold. Because 14 tests are conducted, these comparisons are exploratory; small expected counts may make asymptotic p-values unreliable.

1.15 R4c — Operational effectiveness by residence

Operational effectiveness is examined using independent-group comparisons for recoded statement frequency and clarity (Q9_R, Q10_R), employee and loan-related performance (Q31–Q34), and overall management rating (Q37_R), plus independence tests for Q11 and Q12. Among these nine unadjusted comparisons, Q34 (ease of completing loan applications) differs by residence (headquarters mean 3.28 versus elsewhere mean 3.61, Welch p = .025); the remaining item tests are not significant at .05. Because multiple outcomes are tested, the isolated Q34 result warrants caution. These are item-level comparisons rather than a single unvalidated composite.

3 Methodological notes

  • The supplied SPSS dataset contains 128 records; analyses use all records, not the existing FILTER_$ flag.
  • The one-sample t-tests compare item means with the scale midpoint, 3. For the recoded Q37_R this is a reference point, but the no-opinion category requires caution.
  • For Q9_R, a midpoint of 3 is not assumed; the goodness-of-fit test uses observed categories.
  • Chi-square tests are sensitive to small expected cell counts; review Min_expected in the R4b output.
  • The analysis does not repeat factor analysis or reliability analysis from the prior assignment.
